The 2026–27 flu vaccine has arrived. The CDC recommends a yearly flu shot for everyone six months and older, and especially for:

- adults 65 and older
- pregnant patients
- people with asthma, diabetes, heart disease or other chronic conditions
- healthcare workers and caregivers

Flu shots are covered by most insurance plans at no cost to you. Please bring your **insurance card** and let us know about any allergies.
